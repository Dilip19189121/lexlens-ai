Article 01 Operational Charter
document_scanner

1. Scope of Service & Operational Purpose

LexLens AI provides automated analytical review functions exclusively designed for non-commercial consumer education, plain-English clause summarization, and civic risk mitigation. The Service inspects user-provided contractual texts (such as residential lease agreements, freelance statements of work, independent contractor agreements, and standard employment offers) to execute:

  • check_circle Clause Parsing: Tokenization of contractual jargon into conversational semantic units.
  • check_circle Heuristic Anomaly Flagging: Cross-comparison against documented abusive patterns such as unilateral indemnity shifts, non-compete overreaches, mandatory binding arbitration without opt-outs, and unpermitted deposit forfeiture clauses.
  • check_circle Multi-lingual Plain Summaries: Civic accessibility output translations into regional dialects to assist first-generation immigrants and historically disenfranchised signers.

The platform does not execute contracts, maintain signature ledgers, escrow deposits, or arbitrate formal grievances between contracting parties.

4. Zero-Storage & Ephemeral Memory Protocol

In strict adherence to civic-tech data minimization standards, LexLens AI enforces a deterministic Zero-Retention Policy across all public tiers:

Article 05 Technical Disclosures
psychology

5. Accuracy, Hallucination Risks & AI Nuances

Automated analytical systems operate through probabilistic neural models. Users acknowledge and expressly accept the inherent technical parameters of computational analysis:

  • info Jurisdictional Drift: County ordinances, municipal rent stabilization laws, and state-specific statutory amendments develop more rapidly than model parameter refreshes.
  • info Potential False Flags & Omissions: While our risk engine prioritizes high-confidence pattern detection, an AI scanner may fail to identify an atypical onerous clause or flag standard statutory boilerplates as caution items.
  • info Civic Decision Autonomy: LexLens AI does not dictate whether you sign, negotiate, or decline an agreement. Any decision taken post-analysis is at the user's sole discretion and independent evaluation.
